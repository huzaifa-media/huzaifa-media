Your videos, published on autopilot.

Huzaifa Media is a personal scheduling tool that auto-publishes your own videos to your own TikTok account at the times you choose — every day, without lifting a finger.

Connect your TikTok

📅 Set your schedule

Pick the times of day you want to post. The scheduler handles the rest — 8am, 12pm, 4pm, 8pm, or whatever fits your audience.

🎬 Queue your videos

Upload your video files with your own captions. They wait in your personal queue until their slot arrives.

🚀 Direct publishing

At each scheduled time, Huzaifa Media publishes straight to your TikTok profile via TikTok's official Content Posting API.

How it works

1
Connect TikTok securely.
Log in with TikTok's official Login Kit. Your credentials never touch our servers — TikTok handles the login.
2
Upload videos & write captions.
Add your own original videos and captions to your posting queue.
3
Pick posting times.
Choose daily slots. A scheduled job publishes each video directly to your profile.

Questions

Who is this for?

Creators who post consistently and want their own videos published on a reliable daily schedule.

Do you access anyone else's account?

No. Huzaifa Media connects to one TikTok account — yours — and only publishes content you provide.

Is my data shared or sold?

Never. See the Privacy Policy.

What permissions does it need?

Basic account info to log you in, plus video upload and publishing scopes so it can post your scheduled videos.
